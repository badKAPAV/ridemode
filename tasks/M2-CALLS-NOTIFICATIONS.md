# M2 Calls and Notifications widgets

TASK ID: M2-CALLS-NOTIFICATIONS
REQUIREMENTS: M2-CALLS-001, M2-NOTIFY-001 feed slice, M2-QA-001 and shared M1-WIDGET-001.
STATUS: IN_PROGRESS pending real-device, restricted-grant and notification tests. User chose private APK now, with Google Play later; see ADR-039.
SCOPE: Show up to five newest system call-log records with cached contact name, available number, type/date and confirmed direct-call action. Do not store a separate call record. If restricted call-log access cannot be granted, show that history cannot be used. Show up to ten current active notifications from the existing listener; messages always sort first, with avatar/initials, sender/title and up to two body lines; only high-importance nonmessages appear. Each widget has one child scroll area, 4×5 minimum, 6×5 default and a 12-column/device-row maximum. Existing smaller placements show a resize prompt. Clear content on removal/revocation. Settings explains grants.
FILES: `data/{CallHistoryRepository,NotificationFeedRepository}.kt`, `service/RideMediaListenerService.kt`, `feature/widgets/{CallHistoryWidget,NotificationFeedWidget,WidgetCard}.kt`, shared Ride/editor/ViewModel/Settings/Access, manifest, tests and docs.
DEPENDENCIES: Android's hard-restricted READ_CALL_LOG grant for private APK, CALL_PHONE only at confirmed direct-call use, existing user-granted notification listener. No separate call/notification content database or extra listener service.
RISKS: The private installer may refuse READ_CALL_LOG. Google Play distribution is blocked by this permission until replaced or genuine default-handler eligibility is implemented. Some notifications have missing/redacted content or no avatar; importance and active status change. Calls and messages are sensitive; list data stays in memory and the Calls repository clears on pause.
ACCEPTANCE: Five newest system Recents appear in one scroll with names/numbers where provided. Hidden or nonstandard numbers have no call action. Confirmation and use-time CALL_PHONE precede direct calling; emergency numbers open the system dialer. Denied/restricted call log shows unavailable. Ten current notifications appear in one scroll; messages lead regardless of importance, other entries require high importance, bodies truncate at two lines, avatar falls back to initials. Updates, ranking changes, removal, grant and revocation update/clear promptly. Unit/lint/APK/docs/diff checks pass. Per-app notification rules and popups remain separate M2 work.
VERIFICATION: JDK 17 `./gradlew --no-daemon clean :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:lintDebug :app:assembleDebug`; `bash scripts/verify-docs.sh`; `git diff --check`; real-device restricted grant, five-row Recents, direct call, emergency handoff, message/high-priority ordering, avatar, long text, scroll, removal and revoke checks. Until device scenarios pass, both tasks remain IN_PROGRESS.
